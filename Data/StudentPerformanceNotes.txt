### Mary
Strengths: Mary shows excellent creativity and produces well-structured written work. She reads with strong comprehension and expresses her ideas clearly and confidently in essays and class discussions.
Development Areas: Mary struggles with numerical reasoning and multi-step mathematical problems. She often needs extra time and support to work through calculations confidently, and can lose confidence when a problem has several steps.
Recommended Actions: Provide Mary with additional practice on numerical problem-solving, using visual aids and step-by-step worked examples. Pair her with a peer mentor for mathematics and encourage regular short practice sessions rather than long ones.

### John

### David
